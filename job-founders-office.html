About the role

Work across the business to turn open-ended problems into working systems. You’ll help the founding team connect customer needs, internal operations, and new opportunities.

What you’ll do

Coordinate projects, improve day-to-day workflows, support customer onboarding, research new opportunities, and keep important details from falling through the cracks. Use AI tools and lightweight automation to make recurring work simpler.

What you bring

Strong organization, clear communication, and good judgment when priorities change. You enjoy learning unfamiliar areas, taking ownership, and moving from an idea to a practical result.
