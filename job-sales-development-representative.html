Sales Development Representative

About the role

Help ambitious businesses discover Hypesonic. You’ll research prospective customers, open thoughtful conversations, and turn interest into qualified meetings.

What you’ll do

Build targeted prospect lists, write personalized outreach, qualify inbound and outbound opportunities, and keep the pipeline organized. Work with the founding team to test messaging and improve the handoff from first conversation to sales meeting.

What you bring

Clear writing, curiosity about businesses, and the persistence to follow through. You’re comfortable speaking with prospective customers, learning new tools, and using feedback to improve your approach.
