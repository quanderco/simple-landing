Title: Founding Software Engineer

Skills: Founding Software Engineer

Status: Open

Job Description:

Responsible for the full product and engineering lifecycle for the company’s software platform, from requirements gathering through design, development, testing, deployment, and ongoing iteration. Track monthly engineering goals tied to product milestones and revenue outcomes. Deliver working software in regular, predictable increments while communicating progress, risks, and blockers to leadership. Define role requirements, write job descriptions, source candidates, conduct interviews and make hiring recommendations. Use skills including full-stack software development (frontend and backend), database design, API development, AI/ML integration, automated testing, cloud deployment, and UI/UX design fundamentals.

Requires a U.S. Bachelor of Science Degree in Software Engineering, Information Technology, or Computer Engineering. Must have the authority to work permanently in the U.S.

Location: San Francisco, CA

Term: FULL TIME - 40 Hours per week/9:00am-5:00pm
